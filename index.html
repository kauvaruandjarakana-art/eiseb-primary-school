ABOUT US

At the Heart of the Eiseb Community

Eiseb Primary School is located in the eastern part of Namibia, in the Omaheke Region, Otjombinde Constituency, and in the heart of the Eiseb Community.

Since 1998, the school has been committed to providing a strong educational foundation and a supportive learning environment for its learners from Pre-Primary to Grade 7.

OUR HISTORY

A Proud Community School Since 1998

Eiseb Primary School has proudly served the Eiseb Community since 1998. Situated in the Otjombinde Constituency of the Omaheke Region, the school continues to play an important role in providing foundational and primary education to learners in the community.

As the school grows, this website will preserve and share its history, achievements, school activities and important milestones for learners, parents, staff and the wider community.
